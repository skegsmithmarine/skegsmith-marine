Full listing description.

Low hours 2023 Yamaha F150LC four stroke outboard. This specific configuration is legendary for its mechanical reliability and straightforward maintenance. Freshwater exclusive its entire life. Compression tested at a very healthy 185 PSI across all four cylinders. Engine was thoroughly flushed after every use and shows zero signs of corrosion on the steering tube or lower unit.
