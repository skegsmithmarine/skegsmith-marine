SELLER-PROVIDED DETAILS

Full listing description.

The 2022 Yamaha F200XB is described in the seller listing as a 200 HP inline four-cylinder outboard with 215 reported engine hours and a 25-inch extra-long shaft. The seller says it recently received a full 100-hour service. The work listed includes a new OEM water-pump impeller, replacement spark plugs, high-pressure fuel filters and fresh lower-unit gear oil.

The seller reports that the balancer assembly was inspected and remained in factory condition, and that the fuel injectors were checked for clean spray patterns. According to the listing, the engine was used on a pontoon on a calm lake and idles smoothly. A minor superficial scratch is noted on the port-side cowling; the seller otherwise describes the paintwork as being in very good condition.
