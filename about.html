WHO WE ARE

Practical marine support.
A personal approach.

At Skegsmith Marine, we believe finding the right boat parts should feel straightforward and getting help should feel personal.

We’re committed to exceptional customer service and to helping you choose marine parts that suit your boat and your needs. We want you to feel informed about your options and satisfied with your experience from the first conversation onward.

Our range includes outboard engines, propellers, steering systems and more, with products sourced from established brands for quality and durability. We also provide repair, maintenance and servicing for outboard engines.
